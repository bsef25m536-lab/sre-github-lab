Requirements documentation for a Student Task Tracker



\# Student Task Tracker



\## Overview

Student Task Tracker is a simple project designed to help students manage their academic coursework and assignments. It allows students to record their tasks, view their pending work, and monitor their completion progress. The main purpose is to improve time management and help students complete their academic activities on time.



\## Scope

\- In scope: Students can add new coursework tasks.

\- In scope: Students can view their recorded tasks.

\- In scope: Students can mark tasks as completed.

\- Out of scope: Fee payments and financial transactions.

\- Out of scope: Automatic grading of assignments.



\## Stakeholders

\- Student: Records academic tasks and monitors their progress.

\- Instructor: Reviews students' academic task requirements and provides guidance.

\- Project Maintainer: Maintains project documentation and manages updates.



\## Functional Requirements

\- FR-01: The system shall allow students to create a new task by entering its title.

\- FR-02: The system shall display all tasks recorded by the student.

\- FR-03: The system shall allow students to mark a task as complete.



\## Nonfunctional Requirements

\- NFR-01: The system shall display a list of 100 stored tasks within 2 seconds in at least 95 out of 100 trials on the agreed test device.

\- NFR-02: All task-related operations shall be accessible using a keyboard alone.



\## Acceptance Criteria

\- FR-01: When a student enters the title "Read Chapter 1" and saves it, the task shall appear in the task list.

\- FR-02: When two tasks are saved, opening the task list shall display both task titles.

\- FR-03: When a student marks an incomplete task as complete, its status shall change to Complete.



\## Assumptions and Open Questions

\- Assumption A-01: Students will have access to a computer or mobile device to manage their tasks.

\- Question Q-01: Should students be able to set deadlines for their tasks?

\- Question Q-02: Which device and environment will be used to test the system's performance?
